# Building dedicated capacity for responsible tech in your team

The following offers ideas for the types of responsibilities that build dedicated internal capacity for responsible tech, supporting the long-term sustainability and institutionalisation of [these principles](../principles/). While the objective is that all team members should internalise and apply responsible technology, we know that it sometimes takes dedicated support and capacity to make this happen in reality.&#x20;

These are responsibilities to be assigned, not necessarily mandatory job titles to be created. Smaller teams can distribute them across existing roles rather than hiring new ones. This guidance should be exactly that, guidance. Please tailor it to suit your team's existing capabilities, resources and organisational size.&#x20;

{% hint style="info" icon="user-group-crown" %}
Large organisations may consider creating an ethics team so that ethics responsibilities can be assigned across the jobs spectrum and not fall to one or two individuals.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

#### **Leadership**&#x20;

* **Chief Ethics & Justice Officer**
  * Oversee the ethical implications of technology projects, ensuring alignment with public values and ethical standards.
  * Develop ethical guidelines and frameworks for technology deployment and use.
* **Chief Data Officer**
  * Ensure ethical data governance practices.
  * Promote data transparency and accountability.
  * Oversee the ethical use of data analytics and AI.
* **Chief Technology Officer**
  * Lead the development of ethical technology strategies.
  * Ensure technology solutions align with ethical standards.
  * Promote a culture of responsible tech innovation.
* **Procurement Manager**
  * Apply ethical criteria in vendor selection and contracting.
  * Ensure transparency and fairness in procurement processes.
  * Advocate for responsible supply chain practices.

#### **Data and Privacy**

* **Data Protection Officer (DPO)**
  * Ensure compliance with data protection laws and regulations.
  * Safeguard personal data and address privacy concerns.
  * Build trust around data practices.

#### **Design**

* **Community Engagement Specialist**
  * Facilitate communication and engagement between the technology team and the community.
  * Understand community needs, address concerns, and foster public trust in technology initiatives.
* **Accessibility Specialist**
  * Ensure digital services comply with accessibility standards.
  * Make technology usable by the widest possible audience, including people with disabilities.
* **Trust and Safety Officer**
  * Develop policies and practices to ensure user trust and safety.
  * Monitor and address ethical risks related to user interaction, including misinformation, abuse, and harassment.
  * Advocate for user rights and protection within technology platforms.
* **UX Designer**
  * Design inclusive and accessible user interfaces.
  * Consider the ethical implications of design choices on user behaviour.
  * Engage users in the design process to understand diverse needs.

#### **Delivery**

* **Algorithmic Accountability Manager**
  * Oversee the development and deployment of algorithms, ensuring transparency, fairness, and accountability.
  * Conduct regular audits of algorithms for bias and unintended consequences.
  * Engage with stakeholders to address concerns.
* **Data Scientist**
  * Apply ethical considerations in data analysis and model development.
  * Ensure transparency in data sources and methodologies.
  * Advocate for the responsible use of predictive analytics.
  * Apply approved standards in data collection, use, and storage.
* **Product Manager**
  * Integrate ethical considerations into product design and development.
  * Balance business objectives with ethical tech practices.
  * Facilitate stakeholder engagement to understand ethical implications.
* **Software Engineer**
  * Implement coding practices that prioritise security and privacy.
  * Contribute to the development of responsible technology solutions.
  * Engage in continuous learning on ethical tech practices.
* **Project Manager**
  * Ensure ethical standards are met in project execution.
  * Manage risks related to ethical concerns.
  * Incorporate ethics into project timelines and deliverables.

{% hint style="info" %}
Remember, these are ideas for incorporating responsible technology responsibilities into your organisation. Evaluate carefully how to approach this. Customise it to your existing roles. You need to choose how to apply these in your context and bearing in mind, your organisations' constraints.&#x20;
{% endhint %}
