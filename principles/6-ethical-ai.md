# (7) Prioritise fairness, non-discrimination and ethics throughout the pipeline

Ensure the data used in tools, models and other digital solutions, including AI systems is accurate, reliable and representative and that the systems built on it treat people fairly. We want to prevent algorithmic bias, eliminate discriminatory outcomes, and ensure that the products that we build treat all individuals and communities equitably.

* Interrogate whether an automated or AI-driven approach is the right intervention at all, not just whether it can be built fairly
* Critically evaluate datasets, AI algorithms and other tools for potential bias, underrepresentation, and proxy variables that could lead to discriminatory outcomes against protected groups. Actively put strategies in place to mitigate what you find
* Ensure AI applications and tools are developed and used in ways that promote fairness, justice and non-discrimination, not just technical accuracy
* Maintain human oversight and control. This is especially applicable in AI decision-making mechanisms and in high-stakes or critical applications
* Give people a way to understand, question or appeal decisions a system makes about them

**Questions to ask yourself:**

1. Have we audited our AI/Logic for bias against race, gender, or age in the past 6 months?
2. Have we developed and deployed AI and tools in a way that safeguards user privacy and security?
3. If the AI starts producing harmful or nonsensical outputs, do we have a manual "override" or "kill switch" ready to deploy immediately?
