# (9) Be clear, be open, be transparent

Communicate how your technology works, explain decision-making algorithms, and avoid opaque systems.

* Provide clear and accessible explanations of how the technology works and its intended purpose pitched to the user e.g. decision-maker, implementer, developer.
* Offer transparency into decision-making algorithms, avoiding black-box models, if possible. Where proprietary systems make that unavoidable, document how the algorithm or model was instructed to work with the data
* Maintain clear documentation of the production process for future reference and accountability.
* Give users a clear way to raise questions or flag when something isn't clear or transparent
* Make documentation genuinely findable and readable. Consider where it's stored and how easily someone can actually locate and understand it
* Set milestone check-ins to confirm that documentation still matches reality



**Questions to ask yourself:**

1. Could someone outside the project understand how and why this technology makes its decisions?
2. If we're using a black-box or proprietary model, have we documented how it's been instructed to work with our data?
3. Does our documentation still match what's actually happening, or has it drifted out of date?
