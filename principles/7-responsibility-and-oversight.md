# (8) Take responsibility and be accountable

Establish clear ownership for the consequences of your technology and give users a way to challenge or appeal decisions that affect them.&#x20;

* Define clear roles and responsibilities within the team so accountability for decisions is never ambiguous.&#x20;
* Build in escalation paths both within the project team and to external stakeholders so concerns can reach someone removed enough from the project to act independently
* Proactively identify and mitigate risks throughout development and deployment processes
* Conduct regular internal and external audits using a defined assessment framework that specifies when audits happen and who runs them
* Establish clear procedures for responding to user and stakeholder concerns or feedback about the technology's impact
* Keep policies explainable and transparent, so people can understand how and why decisions are made
* Give people affected by automated decisions a clear way to contest or appeal them



**Questions to ask yourself:**

1. Is there a named individual (e.g. Data Officer) who is accountable for system failures?
2. Do we have a risk management strategy in place?
3. Is there a 3-year budget and/or plan for security patches and hardware maintenance?
