# (6) Minimise your ecological footprint

Consider environmental and long-term societal costs across your tech life-cycle including energy use, e-waste, and the resource extraction that goes into hardware.

* Integrate resource management practices across the entire tech lifecycle, not just at the design or disposal stage
* Optimise hardware and software selection for minimal resource consumption and energy efficiency
* Consider the environmental impact of materials used, and prioritise sustainable production practices where you have a choice of suppliers or manufacturers
* Design for longevity and easy repairability to extend product lifespan and reduce e-waste
* Provide clear guidelines for responsible end-of-life disposal and recycling



**Questions to ask yourself:**&#x20;

* Have we optimised our hardware and software for minimal resource consumption and energy efficiency?
* Have we chosen hosting/cloud providers with high renewable energy commitment?&#x20;
* Have we designed this to be repaired, upgraded or responsibly disposed of, rather than replaced?
